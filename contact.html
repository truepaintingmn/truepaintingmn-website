Texting is usually fastest

I'm often up a ladder with paint on my hands and can't get to the phone. A text gets a reply as soon as I'm down. If you send a couple of photos of the room along with it, I can often give you a rough range before I even come out.

What happens next

1. You reach out. 2. I reply, usually the same day, and we pick a time. 3. I come look at the space — about 20 minutes, no pitch. 4. You get a written price and a paint list. 5. You think about it for as long as you want.
