Straight answers

What I don't do

I'd rather turn work down than take it on and do a mediocre job. If you need any of these, tell me anyway — I'll point you toward the right trade.

Exterior painting

Siding, decks, fences and staining. Interior only, on purpose — it's what I'm good at and it's what I want to keep being good at.

Kitchen cabinet refinishing

Doing cabinets right needs a spray setup and a controlled space. A brushed cabinet job looks fine for a year and then chips. I'd rather say no.

Ceilings & doors

Not right now. I keep the focus on walls and trim so every job gets full attention. Ask anyway — I can point you to someone good, or time it so both happen in the same visit.

Popcorn ceiling removal

Textured ceilings from before the mid-1980s can contain asbestos. That's work for a licensed abatement contractor, not a painter.

Major drywall & water damage

Big holes, ceiling replacement and water-damaged board need a drywall or restoration contractor first. Painting over a problem doesn't fix it.
